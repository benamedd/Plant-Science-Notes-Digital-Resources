About

A personal space for sharing scientific knowledge

Plant Science Notes brings together material developed from academic teaching, research experience and continuing independent scientific work. The aim is simple: to make useful knowledge, practical methods and scientific resources easier to consult and share.

From plant health to digital science

The resources cover plant pathology, plant virology, microbiology, disease epidemiology, molecular methods, bioinformatics, sequencing and plant viral metagenomics.

For learning and reference

The site is intended for students, researchers, diagnosticians and anyone interested in plant health. It is a personal scientific resource site, not a university laboratory or institutional research centre.
